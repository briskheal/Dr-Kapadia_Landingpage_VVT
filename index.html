Advanced Treatment Options

We specialize in minimally invasive, painless procedures that require no hospital stay and offer immediate recovery.

VenaSeal (Medical Glue)

The latest non-thermal, non-surgical treatment using a specialized medical adhesive to seal diseased veins. 100% painless with no anesthesia required.

Laser treatment (EVLA) is a minimally invasive procedure where a small laser fiber is inserted into the diseased vein. The laser energy seals the vein shut, and the body naturally redirects blood flow to healthy veins.
